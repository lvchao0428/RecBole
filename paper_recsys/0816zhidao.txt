最最后我记得第十三周课上说过，RQ的模式在Section 4是一个很好的写法，但是在Abstract里面或者Introduction里面写需要注意措辞。现在只是简单罗列RQ的话看上去有点生硬，如果我写的话，我会像下面这样写：

Text features are widely used to enrich sequential recommenders, but comparisons among lexical, single-view LLM, and prompt-based multi-view representations are often conducted under evaluation protocols that permit temporal overlap. We conduct a leakage-aware controlled study of ID-only, cutoff-fit TF-IDF, single-view Qwen2.5-7B, and four-view LLM representations under full-ranking global temporal splitting (GTS) on three Amazon datasets, using GTS-Last as the primary protocol, GTS-Random as a robustness check, and clean leave-one-out (LOO) as a contrast.

Three findings emerge. First, evaluation protocol changes conclusions: text consistently improves over ID-only on warm targets, but the relative ordering of TF-IDF, single-view LLM, and multi-view LLM changes across Last, Random, and LOO, even when Last and Random evaluate the same trained checkpoints. Second, no text representation is robustly dominant across temporal windows; temporal shift reduces absolute performance while preserving near-ties among text variants. Third, additional prompt views provide limited marginal value because they are highly redundant: inter-view CKA reaches xxx-xx, the concatenated representation uses only xx-xx% of its nominal effective rank, and leave-one-view-out has little downstream effect. In contrast, TF-IDF is geometrically distinct from LLM views (CKA \approx xxxx).

These results suggest that evaluation protocol and representation redundancy should be diagnosed before attributing gains to increasingly complex LLM text pipelines.
