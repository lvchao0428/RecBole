Thank you for the detailed reviews. We respectfully clarify four factual points. (1) The experiments cover two Amazon retail domains(Beauty and Toys&Games), not a single dataset, under full-ranking evaluation with popularity-stratified analysis(Sec.4.1, Table1). (2)The paper does include text-feature baselines: SASRec+TF-IDF and SASRec+TF-IDF+LLM(single-view)(Sec.4.2). The layered/shared-backbone design is intentional: it isolates the incremental value of each text component rather than confounding results with cross-architecture differences. (3) Our cold-start setting studies low-interaction items, not zero-interaction unseen-item insertion. Sec.4.1 defines new=[1,3) training interactions. In Table3/Sec.5 we already state that on Beauty MV-Align narrows(not exceeds) the HR_new gap to ID-only, while on Toys it exceeds it; it achieves the best NDCG_new/MRR_new on both datasets. (4) Alignment and the cross-network trade-off are explicitly analyzed in Table4/Sec.6: -Align lowers HR@10 from 6.58 to 6.41 and HR_new@10 from 2.07 to 1.83, while -Cross raises HR@10 from 6.58 to 7.22 but reduces MRR@10 from 3.72 to 3.31 and NDCG@10 from 4.40 to 4.23. We agree that additional external baselines/backbones would strengthen the paper, but these are scope limitations rather than missing details.
